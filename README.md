## ❓ Why Do We Need This Tool?
When embedding a free Zapier Chatbot (`https://your-bot.zapier.app`) directly into Google Sites using an `<iframe>`, Zapier blocks the connection with:
> **`your-bot.zapier.app refused to connect.`**

This tool solves the problem by generating a customizable, responsive **AI Customer Service Card** that launches the chatbot in a dedicated floating popup window (`window.open`), which works 100% of the time on all Zapier plans!

## 🎓 Student Guide (學生使用指南)

### Step 1: Open the Generator (開啟產生器)
Visit **[https://digimarketingai.github.io/ai-chatbot-embed/](https://digimarketingai.github.io/ai-chatbot-embed/)**.

### Step 2: Customize Your AI Panel (自訂你的 AI 面板)
1. **Your Chatbot URL:** Paste your Zapier Chatbot link (e.g., `https://tai-yau-ai.zapier.app`).
2. **Icon / Logo:** Pick an Emoji (`🤖`, `💬`, `🛍️`) or paste an image URL for your brand logo.
3. **Brand Title & Description:** Enter your company name, slogan, and welcome instructions.
4. **Quick Color Themes:** Choose a color preset (Ocean Blue, Emerald, Royal Purple, etc.) or pick custom brand colors.
5. **Test It:** Click the button in the **Live Interactive Preview** on the right to test your popup chat window!

### Step 3: Embed into Google Sites (嵌入至 Google Sites)
1. Click the blue **`📋 Copy HTML Embed Code for Google Sites`** button.
2. Open your **Google Sites** editor.
3. In the right sidebar, click **Insert (插入)** &rarr; **Embed (嵌入)**.
4. Select the **Embed code (嵌入程式碼)** tab.
5. Paste your copied HTML code, click **Next (下一步)**, and click **Insert (插入)**.
6. Resize the card frame on your Google Site and click **Publish (發佈)**!

## 🔄 How to Edit Your Panel Later (如何匯入及修改舊代碼)
Click the **`📥 Import Existing URL / Code`** button at the top right of the builder. You can paste:
* Your previously copied **HTML Embed Code**, OR
* Your **Direct Embed URL**, OR
* Any **Zapier Chatbot URL**

The builder will automatically load your settings so you can update them!
